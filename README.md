## The Science Behind Kratom Alkaloids

Understanding kratom's active compounds provides insight into how capsules produce their effects.

### Primary Alkaloids

Kratom contains over 40 alkaloids, with two predominating:

**Mitragynine** (60-66% of total alkaloid content):
- Primary active compound
- Interacts with opioid receptors (primarily mu and delta)
- Contributes to both stimulating and sedating effects depending on dose
- Also affects serotonin and dopamine systems

**7-hydroxymitragynine** (1-2% of total alkaloids):
- More potent than mitragynine at opioid receptors
- Primarily responsible for sedating, analgesic effects
- Higher concentrations in red vein strains
- Metabolite of mitragynine (body converts some mitragynine to this compound)

### Secondary Alkaloids

Lesser-known compounds contribute to kratom's overall effect profile:

**Speciogynine**: Third most abundant alkaloid, contributing to muscle relaxation
**Paynantheine**: Smooth muscle relaxant properties
**Speciociliatine**: May contribute to sedating effects
**Corynantheidine**: Potentially affects mood and cognition

The **entourage effect** suggests these alkaloids work synergistically, producing more complex effects than isolated compounds alone.

### Mechanism of Action

Kratom alkaloids interact with multiple receptor systems:

**Opioid receptors**: Mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine act as partial agonists at mu-opioid receptors, producing effects distinct from full agonists like prescription opioids.

**Adrenergic receptors**: Interaction with alpha-2 adrenergic receptors may contribute to sedating effects.

**Serotonin receptors**: Some alkaloids affect serotonin pathways, potentially influencing mood.

**Dopamine receptors**: Kratom may modulate dopamine activity, contributing to mood and motivation effects.

This complex receptor interaction profile explains kratom's diverse, dose-dependent effects.
